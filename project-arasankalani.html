About This Project

Jasper Homes' Arasankalani layout was one of our landmark residential projects — a boutique development of 11 premium plots that was fully sold out within months of launch. The project set a benchmark for clear documentation, quality infrastructure, and transparent transactions in the area.

All 11 plot owners have received their registered sale deeds with zero legal complications. The layout today is a thriving residential pocket with multiple villas under construction — a testament to Jasper Homes' commitment to building communities, not just selling plots.
