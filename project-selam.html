About This Project

Jasper Elakhiya Gardens presents an exclusive plotted development in Pallipatti Village, Salem, a rapidly developing residential destination with excellent growth potential. With 43 premium plots ranging from 1122 to 2000 sq.ft., this project offers the perfect opportunity to build your dream home in a peaceful and well-connected environment.

The layout features well-planned internal blacktop roads, clear title documentation, and legally compliant plots with North-facing, South-facing, and Corner plot options available. Surrounded by a serene natural setting and established residential neighborhoods, Jasper Elakhiya Gardens combines comfort, convenience, and long-term investment value.
